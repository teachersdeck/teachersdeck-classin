# TeachersDeck v0.21.0 beta

## What's new
- **Stats & XP → 📈 My Stats & XP.** Renamed everywhere it shows (the widget, the XP pill's tooltip, the Documents hint and the badge hint; English and Vietnamese: *Thống kê & XP của tôi*). It's your own stats — students' are in Student Stats.
- **New widget: 🎁 Reward Points.** The rewards catalog moved here from Cloud Class. Add a reward (name, cost, optional description), **Hide / Show** it from students, delete it (with a confirmation), and see **how many students can afford each one**. Beside it: **Recently redeemed** (what students spent points on) and **Class points** for every student. It uses the same cloud calls as before — nothing new is stored or sent. It takes a free dock slot on upgrade (or lives in the app tray).
- **Cloud Class, reorganised.** A class header, one-click shortcuts to **Reward Points, Student Stats, Notes Home and Class Builder**, the two join codes side by side (compact), the roster — where points are awarded, as before — and a collapsed **⚙️ Class settings** section (ranking visibility, sign out). No more rewards editor.
- **Notes Home, as a two-pane mail / messages layout.**
  - **Left:** your students with a search box. Tick a student to add them as a recipient; click a student to open their conversation. Each row shows their last note, when, and an unread count; **All notes** sits at the top.
  - **Right:** the conversation as message bubbles, oldest to newest with day separators — 🙂 notes in amber, 🙁 notes in red. **✓ Sent** becomes **✓✓ Read** once a parent opens it. A class-wide note reads as **one** message ("To: 12 students · 7 of 12 read"); an unread note can still be taken back.
  - **Composer under it,** email-style: **To:** (recipient chips you can remove), **From:** (your "sign as" name), the message, 🙂/🙁, a counter and Send (**Ctrl/⌘+Enter** also sends). Same rules as before: pick a mood, and a 🙁 note needs text.
  - The ✉️ button on a roster row still opens it with that student ticked and their conversation shown.
  - Narrow windows stack the panes. Notes Home's default size is 840 × 600, and an existing saved size is widened once so the layout has room.

## Notes
- Awarding and removing points still happens on each student's row in Cloud Class.
- Languages other than English and Vietnamese show English for the new text.
