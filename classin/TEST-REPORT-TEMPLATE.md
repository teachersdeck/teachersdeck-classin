# What to send back after a ClassIn test

1. **Run `classin-check.html` inside ClassIn's browser**, with a second device joined as a student. Work through the 8 checks. Press **Show report → Copy** and paste the whole thing into the chat.
2. **Add this by hand** (the page can't see it):
   - Computer: Windows or Mac, how old, how much RAM
   - ClassIn version, and your ClassIn plan (does it show a **Browser** tool in the teaching toolbox?)
   - Student device (phone / laptop) and whether they were in the room or online
3. **Screenshots** (from the student device where you can):
   - anything marked ⚠ or ✖ in the report
   - `…/index.html?edition=classin&w=timer&sec=10&go=1` — before, and when the "Time's up" flash appears
   - `…&w=instructions…` (the bilingual steps) on the student device — are all Vietnamese accents there?
   - the Noise Meter after you press Start (or the message it shows)
4. **Run the self-test twice**: once now, once after **closing and reopening** the ClassIn browser (check 6 decides whether settings and Pro licences stick).
5. **One line each**: what felt slow, what was confusing, what a teacher would have asked.

Claude reads the report, fixes what it finds, and sends a new build. Expect 3–6 rounds for Option 1.
